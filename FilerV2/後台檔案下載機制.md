## Routing

```csharp
routes.MapRoute(
    "VirtualDirectoryDocuments",
    "Docs/{*pathInfo}",
    new { controller = "Document", action = "Get" });
```

- **路由名稱**：VirtualDirectoryDocuments
- **URL 模式**：Docs/{*pathInfo}
- **控制器**：Document
- **動作**：Get
- **功能**：處理所有以 "Docs/" 開頭的請求，將其導向 Document 控制器進行檔案存取處理
